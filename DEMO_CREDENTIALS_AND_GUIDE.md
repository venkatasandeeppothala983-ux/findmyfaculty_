## 💻 2. Running Locally on Your Laptop (100% Offline Mode)

If the professor asks you to run it locally on your laptop without internet:

### For Windows:
1. Double-click **`run_windows.bat`**.
2. It will automatically start the server and open **`http://localhost:8080`** in your default browser.

### For Mac / Linux:
1. Open Terminal, navigate to the folder, and run:
   ```bash
   bash run_mac_linux.sh
   ```
2. Or simply run:
   ```bash
   python3 server.py
   ```
   and open **`http://localhost:8080`** in your browser.

## 🎬 4. The 2-Minute Live Evaluation Script (Show this to your professor)

Follow these exact steps during your practical demo:

### Step 1: Opening Screen (Role Selection)
* Show the clean **3-Card Portal** (*Student, Faculty, Admin*).
* Point out the dark/light mode toggle in the top right.
* Click **"⚡ 1-Click Demo Login (Sandeep)"**.

### Step 2: Student Experience
* Show the personalized greeting: *"Good evening, Sandeep 👋"*.
* In the search bar, type `"DBMS"` or select Department `"CSE"`.
* Click on **Dr. Ravi Kumar**'s card (shows 🟢 **AVAILABLE NOW**, 📍 **CSE Block — Room 204**).
* Click the **"🗺️ Get Directions"** button:
  * Show the interactive campus building grid.
  * Show the step-by-step walking guide: *Enter CSE Block → Level 2 → Corridor → Room 204*.
* Click **"My Timetable"** to show Sandeep's III Year CSE timetable.
* Click **"Sign Out"**.

### Step 3: Faculty Self-Service & Real-Time Status Toggle
* Click **"⚡ 1-Click Demo Login (Dr. Ravi Kumar)"**.
* Show the **"Welcome, Dr. Ravi Kumar"** dashboard.
* Click the blue **"⚡ Update Status"** button:
  * Switch status to 🟠 **IN MEETING** or 🟣 **ON DUTY**.
  * Add custom activity note: *"Exam Evaluation in Room 108"*.
  * Click **Save**.
  * Show the status immediately changes with *"Last updated: Just now"*.
* Show **Today's Schedule timeline** with active lecture highlights.
* Click **"Sign Out"**.

### Step 4: Admin Governance & Live Status Wall
* Click **"⚡ 1-Click Demo Login (Admin)"**.
* Show the **6 Central Metric Tiles**:
  * `1,250 Total Students`

  * `41 Currently Teaching`
  * `12 Unavailable`
* Click the **"Live Status"** tab in the navigation bar:
  * Show the real-time table of all university faculty.
  * Click the **1-Click Admin Overrides** (*Free, Meeting, Absent*) to show live control.
* Click the **"Feedback"** tab:
  * Show the student survey analytics with the **4.8 / 5 ⭐ satisfaction rating**.
